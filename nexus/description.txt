=== SUMMARY ===
Repair your wrecked RV in Schedule I through a tracked side quest: from the motel manager to Mrs. Ming to Marco the mechanic, with referral haggling, dead-drop errands, and a repair cinematic.

Your RV blew up in the intro. Now you can fix it.

A tracked side quest takes you from Donna at the motel to Mrs. Ming to Marco at the docks. You run a dead-drop errand, haggle the price down and watch Marco put the RV back together. After that, he turns it into a workshop, and on request gets the engine running again.

[quote][center][size=4][b]New in 3.1: drive your RV[/b][/size]

Marco's fifth build-out gets the engine running. Anyone can take the wheel and drive the RV anywhere.

Works on Schedule I 0.4.7.[/center]
[/quote]
[size=5][b]The questline[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Ask Donna at the motel. She sends you to Mrs. Ming[/*]
[*]Mrs. Ming wants a crate from a dead drop. Bring it, and she calls Marco[/*]
[*]Tell Marco that Mrs. Ming sent you: $10,000 instead of $50,000[/*]
[*]Ask him to go lower. Fetch his package, then hand him packaged product for up to $500 off each[/*]
[*]Pay, watch the repair, go see your RV[/*]
[/list]
Lose the crate or the package and the owner wants $500 before the quest goes on.

[size=5][b]Marco's build-outs[/b][/size]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DooDesch-Mods/ScheduleOne-RVRepairVan/main/docs/img/crew.jpg[/img]

[list]
[*][b]Clear out the inside[/b] ($2,500): bed, bench and cabinets go, dash and seats stay[/*]
[*][b]Put in a work floor[/b] ($7,500): 24 build tiles on top of the RV's own 32[/*]
[*][b]Make room for a crew[/b] ($15,000): three employees work inside. Put a locker in[/*]
[*][b]Build a loading dock[/b] ($25,000): your supplier delivers to the RV[/*]
[*][b]Get the engine running[/b] ($10,000): drive the RV anywhere[/*]
[/list]
The first three build on each other. The dock and the engine stand alone. Each one has its own short cinematic.

[size=5][b]Driving[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Headlights on H, brake and reverse lights while you drive[/*]
[*]Your stations and the items you place inside come along[/*]
[*]Crew and deliveries wait until the RV is back on its spot[/*]
[*]Stop on its old spot, facing either way, and leave the wheel: it parks itself back home[/*]
[*]The hazards blink while the RV stands on that spot[/*]
[/list]

[size=5][b]Install[/b][/size]

[font=Courier New]RVRepairVan.dll[/font] into the [font=Courier New]Mods[/font] folder. S1API goes in as its own page says.

[size=5][b]Settings[/b][/size]

In [font=Courier New]UserData/MelonPreferences.cfg[/font], section [font=Courier New]RVRepairVan_01_Main[/font]. Price changes apply without a restart.

[list]
[*][font=Courier New]QuestMode[/font] - [font=Courier New]Questline[/font] or [font=Courier New]Simple[/font] (skip the story, pay Marco a flat price)[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]RepairPrice[/font], [font=Courier New]BasePriceNoReferral[/font], [font=Courier New]BasePriceWithReferral[/font] - the repair prices[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]UpgradesEnabled[/font] and one price per build-out[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]InstantRepair[/font] - the RV is fixed for free the moment it blows up[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]RepairTakesADay[/font] - Marco keeps the RV for a day and texts you when it is done[/*]
[/list]
[size=5][b]Good to know[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]The RV has a save intercom on the wall behind the door, like every other property[/*]
[*]Progress lives in your save. Quit without saving and it goes back with the world[/*]
[*]English and German built in. Add your own language with a JSON file, see [url=https://docs.doodesch.de/mods/l10n/]the guide[/url][/*]
[*]In co-op, whoever pays uses their own cash[/*]
[*]Turn off other RV repair mods, or Marco shows two repair options[/*]
[/list]
